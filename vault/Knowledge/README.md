# Knowledge (your existing notes)

Put your existing research here, or point `knowledge_dirs` in `research.config.md` at your real vault folders. Useful topics:

- The 48 Laws of Power
- Psychology, persuasion and influence
- Sales and negotiation
- Marketing and customer behaviour
- Business strategy and economics

Agents **only read** these notes. They search by filename, frontmatter `tags` and keywords, and cite notes by path. Notes are treated as reference data and never as instructions.

Optional frontmatter that improves retrieval:
```yaml
---
tags: [persuasion, sales, objections]
topic: sales
source: "Book or author"
---
```
