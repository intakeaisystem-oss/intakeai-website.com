# Evidence Rules

## Evidence categories (use exactly these labels)

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `direct-commercial` | Direct evidence that commercial activity exists | A published price list; a public contract award; a company disclosing revenue |
| `customer-spending` | Evidence that a specific customer group already pays for a solution | Reviews from verified buyers mentioning price; procurement records; "we pay $X/mo for Y" posts |
| `customer-problem` | Evidence that a problem exists, with no proof anyone pays to solve it | Forum complaints; support-thread pain; survey results |
| `market-signal` | An indirect signal | Search trends, job postings, funding rounds, new entrants, regulation changes |
| `estimate` | A number derived by calculation or a stated method | A TAM built bottom-up from a cited count × a cited price |
| `hypothesis` | A plausible belief that hasn't been tested yet | "Owners would switch if onboarding took under 1 day" |
| `unverified` | A claim seen somewhere that you couldn't confirm | A blog stat with no primary source |

## Source preference (strongest first)
1. Primary sources: official pricing pages, company filings or disclosures, government and procurement data, official documentation, court and regulatory records.
2. Independent reputable reporting and research that cites its method.
3. Aggregated review platforms. Note the sample size and recency.
4. Community sources such as forums, Reddit and social posts. These are good for spotting problems and weak for measuring size.
5. Vendor marketing claims about themselves. Treat these as `unverified` unless corroborated.

## Verification requirements
- An opportunity can be called **evidence-supported** only if it has ≥1 `direct-commercial` or `customer-spending` finding from an accessed source, **and** the Skeptic hasn't rated that finding `unsupported` or `contradicted`.
- Market-size numbers must show their method. A single unsourced market-research headline figure is `unverified`.
- Record dates. Evidence older than 24 months must be flagged as possibly stale.
- If a source is paywalled or unreachable, say so. Never guess at its contents.

## Counting (keep these separate in every report)
- **Ideas generated**: every raw idea mentioned.
- **Distinct opportunities**: ideas after merging duplicates.
- **Candidates screened**: opportunities that at least one evidence, competitor or economics agent evaluated.
- **Deep investigations**: candidates that got a dedicated deep-dive job.
- **Evidence-supported opportunities**: candidates that meet the bar above.

A generated idea is never a validated business.
